What the chamfer asymmetry means

Pred → GT mean: 0.356. GT → pred mean: 0.739. The cameras orbit at radius 2.5m and only see part of the 4m floor; GT samples on the unseen edges have no nearby predicted point. The asymmetry is reading the method's coverage, not its faithfulness. Three ways to handle: trim the floor in the scene, mask GT to the visible region via camera frustums, or accept the asymmetric metric as real signal. Open methodology question — see docs/open_questions.md.
